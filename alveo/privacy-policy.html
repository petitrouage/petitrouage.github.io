Alveo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Alveo is a logic puzzle game for Android phones: you reveal the cells of a hive while avoiding hidden hornets, guided by numbers. Each level earns honey to grow your hive.

In short: your game stays on your phone. There is no account and no analytics or crash-reporting tool. Alveo shows Google AdMob ads; in Europe a form first asks for your consent, and you can decline. The "Remove forced ads" purchase goes through Google Play.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your progress: completed levels and stars, honey, hive, garden decorations, bee species discovered, daily challenge streak, hints used today.
  • Your settings: sounds, vibration, and whether the "Remove forced ads" purchase is active.
  • Your privacy choices for advertising, stored by Google's consent tool (see below).

This information does not leave your phone, except through the Android backup described below. The publisher has no access to it.

What the app does not do

No account, no audience measurement or usage analytics, no crash reporting, no sale of data, no chat with other people. Alveo does not ask for your location, contacts, phone number, microphone or camera.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Two kinds of ads. Rewarded ads only appear when you choose to watch them (for example to get a hint, recover a heart or double your honey). Ads between levels appear at most once every three breaks, never during the first five levels. There is never an ad during a puzzle.
  • Data processed by Google. To show ads, Google AdMob may process your phone's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), technical information about the device and app, and your interactions with ads. This processing is governed by Google's policy for partner apps.
  • Your consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a form appears on first launch: you can accept, decline or choose in detail. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, i.e. not based on your profile. No ad is requested before this form has been handled.
  • Change your mind at any time: in Alveo, Settings › Privacy › "Change my privacy choices".
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android settings (Google › Ads).

"Remove forced ads" purchase

  • This one-time purchase removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads remain available, always your choice.
  • Payment goes through Google Play. Alveo never sees your payment method. Google Play sends the publisher the information needed to manage orders (for example the order number, product, price and country). Payment is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • To know whether the purchase is active (including on a new phone, "Restore my purchase" button), the app queries Google Play on your phone.
  • Honey earned in Alveo can never be bought with money.

Backup

If backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android copies Alveo's data to your Google backup space so you can get it back on a new phone. This copy is managed by Google and linked to your Google account; the publisher has no access to it. You can turn this backup off in Android settings. The purchase status is checked again with Google Play at every launch.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Alveo deletes all its data from the phone (or, in Android settings: Apps › Alveo › Storage › Clear data).
  • The backup copy, if any, is managed in Android backup settings and your Google account.
  • For advertising data processed by Google, reset your advertising ID and see your Google account.

Age

Alveo is intended for people aged 13 and over. The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process advertising data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

Your rights

Alveo keeps no file about you: your game is on your phone, where you can erase it yourself. For any question about data the publisher might hold (for example for a purchase), write to jonestown91@gmail.com. You may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, withdraw your consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated and, for an important change, the app lets you know.
